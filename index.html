About

Dedicated to teaching Finance & Accounting disciplines while contributing impactful research bridging academia and industry.

My thesis explores how the target firm’s overall complexity influences the acquiring firm’s post-acquisition short-term, medium-term, and long-term performance, integration, risks, and deal duration. By leveraging large-scale financial datasets from Bloomberg and applying rigorous econometric techniques in R, my thesis aims to make a meaningful and sustained contribution to the M&A and corporate finance literature.
