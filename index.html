Welcome

Greetings! Welcome to my website. I’m a final-year doctoral candidate in Finance & Accounting at the Institute of Management Technology (IMT) Ghaziabad. I have also passed a Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) in Commerce in 2020.

In my doctoral thesis, I am examining the effect of the target firm’s complexity on the acquiring firm’s characteristics.

During my doctoral program, I had the opportunity to visit the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Sydney, Australia, for 45 days as a Junior visiting fellow. I am fortunate to be one of only 2 selected students from India for this research scholarship.

I also had the opportunity to present my work at the conference of FORE School of Management and MDI Gurgaon in November and December 2025, respectively. I have also been selected for the two-day Doctoral Participant-Centered Learning Workshop (DPCL) conducted by Harvard Business Impact Education, Harvard Business School, and Harvard Business School India Research Center in May 2026.

My research interests include

  • Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)
  • Firm’s complexity, performance, and post-acquisition integration.
  • Capital market.
  • Management Controls
  • Employee perceptions and cultural differences.
  • How strategic financial decisions influence firm performance and market outcomes.

Thank you for visiting my website. Feel free to read my work and reach out to me if you have any questions or would like to notify me about any open positions in your research team at fpm22adnankhan@imt.ac.in.
