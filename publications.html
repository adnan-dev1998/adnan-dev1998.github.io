Journal Publications

Supply chain driven herding behavior during COVID-19: evidence of interdependence from India
Journal of Business and Industrial Marketing, 2024 (ABDC=A, ABS=2, Scopus=Q1)

Purpose – This study aims to test the market anomaly of herding behavior driven by the response to supply chain disruptions in extreme market conditions such as those observed during COVID-19.

Recommendation Citation: Khan, A., Sindhwani, R., Atif, M. and Varma, A. (2024), Journal of Business & Industrial Marketing, Vol. 39 No. 8 pp. 1764-1787.

Accounting-analytic capabilities’ impact on management controls and performance.
Journal of Computer Information Systems (ABDC=A, ABS=2, Scopus=Q1)

The study clarifies the linkage between AIS quality, dynamic accounting analytic system capabilities, management control systems, and firm performance.

Recommended citation: Varma, A., Sharma, A., & Khan, A. (2025). Journal of Computer Information Systems, 1-16.

Understanding How Incentive Contracts and Non-financial Performance Measures Impact Cost Control.
Global Business Review (ABDC=B, ABS=1, Scopus=Q1), 2026

The study examines how incentive contracting and non-financial performance measures influence managerial cost-prioritization choices during external volatility.

Benchmarking for organisational competitiveness: a resource-based view perspective
Benchmarking: An International Journal, 2024 (ABDC=B, ABS=1, Scopus=Q1)

The paper investigates the impact of resource-based view applications in management practices on firm profitability and organisational competitiveness.

Simons levers of control disentangled: A quasi-experiment into the competitiveness of hybrid firms.
Management Control, 2023 (ABDC=C)

The study explores how different control levers enable competitiveness in hybrid firms.
