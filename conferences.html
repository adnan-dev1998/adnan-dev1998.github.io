Research Pipeline

  • Thesis brief- Currently working on M&A for my thesis and intend to continue exploring and deepening my research in this field over the long term. By leveraging large-scale financial datasets from Bloomberg and applying rigorous econometric techniques in R, my thesis aims to make a meaningful and sustained contribution to the M&A and corporate finance literature. In particular, I am exploring how the target firm’s overall complexity influences the acquiring firm’s post-acquisition short-term, medium-term, and long-term performance, integration, risks, and deal duration.
  • My research pipeline includes:
    1. Exploring the role of management controls during post-acquisition integration:
    2. Employee and top management perceptions about management control during cultural differences arise due to M&A deals/transactions:
    3. Predicting M&A deal success using an econometrics model and financial indicators, and how does corporate governance shape the M&A outcomes?
